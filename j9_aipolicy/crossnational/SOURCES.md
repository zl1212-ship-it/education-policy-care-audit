# Cross-national data sources

- **Policy text**: Internet Archive Wayback Machine (web.archive.org), dated captures of
  each institution's academic-integrity / honour-code / conduct page and its generative-AI
  guidance page. Each capture is addressed by exact archived URL plus timestamp; see
  `data/snapshot_index.csv`. Curated page URLs per institution: `uk_seeds.csv`,
  `au_seeds.csv`, `ca_seeds.csv`, `recurate_seeds.csv`.
- **UK international-student exposure**: HESA, "Where do HE students come from?", Student
  record 2021/22, Figure 7 (HE student enrolments by HE provider and domicile), archived
  CSV (regenerated by `uk_hesa_get.py` into `uk_hesa.csv`). Share = non-UK domicile / total.
- **Australia international-student exposure**: Australian Government Department of
  Education, Higher Education Statistics 2022, Section 2 (All students), tables 2.5 and 2.6
  (`2022 Section 2 - All students.xlsx`). Share = (all students minus domestic) / all.
- **Canada**: no institution-level international-share series is used; Canadian institutions
  enter the convergence and prevalence analyses but not the exposure gradient.
- **United States** (parent pipeline): IPEDS non-resident-alien enrollment share; see
  `../SOURCES.md`.
