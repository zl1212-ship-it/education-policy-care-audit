# Cross-national extension: UK / Australia / Canada AI-policy panel

Extends the US AI / academic-integrity policy event study (parent directory) to three
further Anglophone systems, for a four-system panel of 82 universities (US 47, UK 14,
AU 8, CA 13). The method matches the US pipeline: dated Internet Archive snapshots of each
institution's academic-integrity and generative-AI guidance pages, scored on the shared
provision lexicon in `build_panel.py`, assembled into an institution-by-quarter panel
around the public release of ChatGPT (2022-11-30).

## Run order

1. Curated page URLs per institution are in `uk_seeds.csv`, `au_seeds.csv`, `ca_seeds.csv`
   (`recurate_seeds.csv` holds re-tries). The assembled, curated frame with exposure is
   committed as `data/sample_frame.csv`.
2. `patient_scale.py` and `patient_round.py <seeds.csv> <COUNTRY>` are throttle-respecting
   Wayback downloaders: they probe the Wayback IP until it recovers, then fetch the exact
   archived captures into `data/snapshots_raw/` (gitignored; regenerated) and append rows to
   `data/snapshot_index.csv`.
3. `build_panel.py` scores each archived snapshot on the provision lexicon and builds the
   quarterly panel (`data/panel.csv`, `data/panel_snapshots.csv`).
4. Exposure (international-student share):
   - `uk_hesa_get.py` downloads the archived HESA Figure 7 (2021/22) CSV to `uk_hesa.csv`
     (gitignored; 18 MB). `uk_exposure.py` fills the UK `intl_share` from it.
   - `au_exposure.py` fills the AU `intl_share` from the Australian Department of Education
     2022 Section 2 tables (`2022 Section 2 - All students.xlsx`).
5. Analysis:
   - `convergence_over_time.py` — mean pairwise AI-text similarity by event quarter,
     within- and cross-system, with an institution-cluster bootstrap on the post-shock
     slope, a topic-strip robustness check, a fixed-cohort (balanced-panel) check, and a
     country-label permutation test. Writes `results_convergence_over_time.csv`.
   - `combined_4system.py` — endpoint provision prevalence by national system.
   - `exposure_gradient.py` — endpoint net restrictiveness vs international-student share,
     per system (UK, AU).
   - `make_convergence_fig.py` — convergence figure.

## Committed data

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `data/sample_frame.csv` | curated institution frame: system, control, Carnegie tier, page URLs, enrollment, international share |
| `data/snapshot_index.csv` | one row per archived capture: institution, page type, Wayback timestamp, local path, bytes |
| `data/panel.csv` | institution-by-quarter provision scores |
| `data/panel_snapshots.csv` | one row per distinct content version (extracted text + scores) |
| `data/coverage.csv` | per-institution snapshot coverage |
| `results_convergence_over_time.csv` | convergence series by event quarter |

`data/snapshots_raw/` (archived HTML) and `uk_hesa.csv` (raw HESA download) are gitignored
and regenerated by the fetchers above. Sources are listed in `SOURCES.md`.
